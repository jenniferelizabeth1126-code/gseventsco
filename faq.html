About the Firm

What is Gold Standard Events Consultancy?

Gold Standard Events Consultancy is a boutique, founder-led event strategy and hospitality consulting firm built on 28 years of corporate event, hotel, restaurant, and venue experience.

We serve three kinds of clients: corporate teams and meeting planners who need a strategic partner or boutique DMC support, private hosts planning luxury celebrations, and restaurants, venues, and hospitality brands that want to grow their event business.

How is that different from a traditional event planning company?

Most event planners focus on logistics. We focus on strategy first, then execution. Before a vendor is hired or a deposit is paid, we build the thinking behind the event: what it needs to accomplish, who it is for, and how every decision supports that.

For corporate clients, that means a true strategic partner rather than another vendor. For venues, it means building or rebuilding the entire events revenue operation, not just offering advice.

Where do you work?

We serve clients nationally and internationally. Our experience spans major markets on both coasts, and we bring trusted local partners into every destination we work in.

Do you plan smaller events?

Yes. We plan events of every size: team building days, office holiday parties, intimate dinner parties, birthday and milestone celebrations, client appreciation events, and brand activations, as well as conferences and 400-guest galas.

Every client gets the same senior-level care. We simply shape the support to fit your guest count, timeline, and budget, whether that is a strategy session, advisory and planning support, or full planning and production.

Who will I actually work with?

Every engagement is led personally by our founder, with a trusted team of planners, coordinators, and specialists brought in as each event requires. You get senior-level attention and a team that scales to the size of your event.

Services

What do Corporate Events & DMC Services include?

We act as a strategic advisor and boutique DMC partner for corporate teams and meeting planners, designing programs that deliver real business outcomes. Services include:

  • Event concept and program development
  • Vendor sourcing, vetting, and negotiation oversight
  • Boutique DMC support for local, national, and international programs
  • Conferences, corporate meetings, and board and executive retreats
  • Incentive programs, team building, and brand activations
  • Attendee engagement strategy and event ROI planning
  • On-site execution oversight and run-of-show management
Do you plan luxury private events?

Yes. We plan and design private events of every size, from intimate dinner parties to 400-guest celebrations: milestone birthdays and anniversaries, holiday and dinner parties, estate and private residence events, galas and fundraisers, and destination celebrations.

We do not plan baby showers, bridal showers, engagement parties, or rehearsal dinners.

What does Venue & Hospitality Consulting include?

We step in as an outsourced Director of Events for restaurants, event venues, and hospitality properties. Engagements may include:

  • Building or rebuilding a private events department
  • Sales team hiring, training, and performance frameworks
  • Revenue strategy and growth planning
  • CRM setup, SOPs, and booking systems
  • Private dining and venue buyout programs
  • Sales coaching and ongoing mentorship for venue teams
Can you help with sales, marketing, and brand strategy?

Yes. We build the sales kits, proposals, marketing plans, and brand positioning that help venues and hospitality brands win clients and fill their event spaces, including website refreshes when the website is what is holding bookings back. See the full service list.

Can you help my brand plan activations and pitch to corporate clients?

Yes. We help food, beverage, and lifestyle brands design brand activations, package them as offers, and build the sales materials and pitch to win corporate clients and event bookings.

What if my event is already in trouble?

That is exactly what Event Rescue is for. Start with a 90-minute Event Rescue Strategy Session for $495, or ask about our 48-Hour Planning Rescue and Emergency Coordination options for events days away.

Do you handle vendor payments on behalf of clients?

No, and this is by design. Clients contract with and pay vendors directly. That removes the traditional DMC markup (typically 20 to 30 percent), keeps your procurement process clean and transparent, and keeps every vendor dollar working for your event.

We handle the strategy, vendor relationships, negotiation oversight, and execution management. Your finance team works directly with the vendors.

Process & Engagement

How does the engagement process work?

Every engagement follows the same foundation:

  • Discovery: a 30-minute conversation to understand your goals, timeline, and whether we are the right fit.
  • Strategy: we build the framework, event concept, vendor roadmap, and success measures around your goals.
  • Execution: with the right partners in place, we oversee every element so you can show up with confidence.
  • Debrief: every engagement closes with a review of what worked, what the results show, and what comes next.
How long does a typical engagement last?

Corporate event strategy projects run from scoping through post-event debrief, typically six weeks to six months depending on complexity. Venue consulting engagements are often longer-term retainers, typically three to twelve months, because building or rebuilding an events department takes sustained effort.

What should I have ready before our first call?

You do not need everything figured out. That is what the discovery call is for. It helps to have a general sense of:

  • The type of event or project you are planning
  • Your approximate timeline
  • A general budget range for the event or the engagement
  • What you hope a consulting engagement will help you accomplish

We will guide the conversation from there.

Pricing & Fees

How is pricing structured?

Pricing is a flat consulting fee or retainer, not an hourly rate and not a percentage of vendor spend. That keeps the relationship clean and aligned: our incentive is the best possible outcome for you, never a bigger vendor bill.

Fees vary with the scope, complexity, and duration of the engagement and are discussed after the discovery call.

Do you work with smaller budgets?

Yes. Every event starts with understanding what you want to accomplish and what you have to work with. From there, we recommend the right level of support, so you are never paying for more than your event needs.

If you are not sure where to start, the best first step is a free 30-minute consultation.

Can you save my organization money compared to a traditional DMC?

Often, yes. Traditional DMCs typically add a 20 to 30 percent management markup on top of vendor invoices. Because clients contract with vendors directly, that markup disappears. You pay a flat consulting fee, and every vendor dollar goes to your event rather than an agency margin.

For Corporate Teams

We already have an internal events team. Why would we need a consultant?

Internal teams are great at what they know. Most still face at least one of these: limited bandwidth in peak planning periods, limited knowledge of an unfamiliar market, a vendor roster that stops at their usual partners, or difficulty raising production quality without raising the budget.

We step in as a seamless extension of your team and fill exactly those gaps, without adding headcount, overhead, or the friction of a large agency.

We already use a DMC. How is this different?

Traditional DMCs add value, but they also carry a significant cost structure and a reason to steer you toward their preferred vendors and packages. Our model is advisory. We have no inventory to sell, no markup to protect, and no vendor exclusivity agreements. Our only incentive is the best program for your goals and budget.

Can you support events in other cities or countries?

Yes. We serve clients nationally and internationally and have supported programs in destinations across the country and abroad, bringing trusted local partners into every market.

For Restaurants & Venues

We do some private events, but we know we are leaving revenue on the table. Can you help?

This is the client we work with most often. Many restaurants and venues have the space, the team, and the demand, but not the systems, sales discipline, or strategy to capture what they are capable of. We have rebuilt departments like this many times, with results that include a 200 percent increase in private event sales and more than $550,000 in catering and event revenue within six months of a hotel re-opening.

We don't have an events team at all. Can you build one from scratch?

Yes. We have built private events programs from the ground up: defining the roles, writing job descriptions, helping with hiring, implementing CRM and booking systems, developing sales scripts and rate cards, and creating the SOPs that let a team run on its own.

We have a team, but they aren't closing consistently. Can you help with sales training?

Yes. Sales training and coaching for event sales teams is one of the most impactful things we do for venues: inquiry response, site visit presentation, proposal strategy, objection handling, follow-up cadence, and closing, all tailored to private events and private dining.
